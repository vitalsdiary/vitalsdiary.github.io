Made for easy logging

Step-by-step entry with big wheels and large text — designed with and for older adults. No tiny forms.

❤️

Measure your heart rate with the camera

Rest a fingertip on the camera and get your pulse in about 15–25 seconds. It records the number for you — nothing more. Not a medical device.

🩸

Blood glucose too

Log glucose in mg/dL or mmol/L with the same big wheels, with optional fasting and after-meal tags.

🔗

Bring in what you already measured

Connect Apple Health or Health Connect and your existing readings come in automatically — read-only, nothing is ever written back.

📈

Trends you can see

7, 30 and 90-day charts with morning/evening averages, covering a full checkup cycle.

📄

PDF report for your doctor

One tap turns your records into a clean report you can print or share at your next visit.

⏰

Gentle reminders

Measurement and medication reminders, plus a simple daily med check-in.

Private by design

No account. No analytics. No cloud. Your readings are stored only on your device — we couldn't see them even if we wanted to.

Vitals Diary records, reminds and displays the values you enter. It does not provide medical interpretation, diagnosis or advice, and is not a medical device. Always consult your healthcare provider.
